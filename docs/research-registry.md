# Gqobonco research registry — public disclosure boundary

Gqobonco is the public-facing research and information home. Its associated private Zalun registry holds unpublished research records and version history. The private registry is not publicly accessible; this public document does not grant access or automatically synchronize its contents.

## Founder-led industrial research

**Uviwe Menyiwe** is the founder of **Production Sentinel** and **SmartWater Guardian**. Both are research-stage inventions; neither has yet been physically built. Funding is sought to acquire materials, deepen technical research, construct prototypes, and conduct controlled testing.

- **Production Sentinel:** proposed modular industrial intelligence architecture for sensing, comparing, understanding, locating, alerting and responding.
- **SmartWater Guardian:** planned first physical proof of Production Sentinel, beginning with a proposed three-zone water-monitoring demonstration.
- **Energy Guardian, Production Intelligence and Maintenance Intelligence:** future architecture extensions; not yet built.

Illustrations, demonstration scripts, estimated budgets and performance targets are plans, not evidence of a completed prototype or validated performance.

## Disclosure and provenance

Public releases should identify the founder, publication date, project status and version, and link to approved supporting materials. Unpublished engineering records and research notes remain in the private registry. Git commit history is a provenance aid, not a substitute for legal intellectual-property protection.

**Integration status:** Gqobonco and Zalun are linked at the documentation level. Automated private-to-public publication has not been enabled.
